## What Doctor Dekho is
tags: about, doctor dekho, kya hai, service
Doctor Dekho lists doctors from hospitals near you. You can see their fees and days, and book an appointment for free;
you pay the doctor's fee at the hospital. Vita helps you find the right doctor.

## Is booking free / do I pay online
tags: free, muft, payment, paise, online payment, refund, charges, booking fee
Finding a doctor and booking an appointment on Doctor Dekho is free. You only pay the doctor's fee at the hospital,
so there is no online payment and no refund.

## Doctor's fee
tags: fees, fee, kitni fees, consultation fee, charges, kitna lagega
Each doctor's consultation fee is shown with their name on Doctor Dekho. You pay it at the hospital.

## Cancel or change an appointment
tags: cancel, reschedule, change appointment, radd, badalna, my appointments
You can see, cancel or change your appointments under 'My appointments' on Doctor Dekho.

## Doctor timings
tags: timing, kitne baje, kab baithte, days, hours, open, khula
Each doctor's days and hours are on their card on Doctor Dekho, and the hospital confirms the exact time.

## Reports and prescriptions
tags: report, prescription, parcha, test result
Prescriptions and test reports come from the doctor and the hospital. Only the doctor can explain what a report means.

## Why my number and OTP are needed
tags: number, otp, privacy, data, details, safe
Your number is used to confirm the appointment and send its details on WhatsApp; the OTP checks the number is yours.
Your name and number are sent to the hospital for the appointment.

## Blood and vaccines
tags: blood, blood bank, donor, vaccine, teeka
Doctor Dekho doesn't arrange blood or vaccines. Please ask the nearest hospital or blood bank directly.

## Emergency numbers
tags: ambulance, emergency number, 108, 112
Ambulance: 108. Any emergency: 112. Mental health support (Tele-MANAS): 14416.
